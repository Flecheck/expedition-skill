# Whether charting an effort establishes and records that it is expected to end

Type: grilling
Status: resolved

## Answer

*Written by this node's worker on attempt 2, and pasted as it wrote it.*

**Yes. Starting an effort settles whether that effort is expected to end, and the map's `## Destination` section
records it in one line.** The human, `verbatim`: *"for 91, ok"*, taken as assent to the recommendation as offered —
yes, and as one line inside the map's existing `## Destination` section. Recorded on this node's `## Interview` as
the manager's reading rather than as the human's words. Not a divergence.

### What is recorded, and where

The `## Destination` section already says what reaching the end of the effort looks like. It gains one sentence
saying whether reaching it is **expected** — *"this effort is expected to end when X"*, or *"this effort is not
expected to end; it runs continuously."* Nothing new is created: no field, no section, no file, and no per-effort
setting to maintain. It is changed later like any other map item, by the `Change the map` procedure.

### Why this and not something else

Three grounds, and the first is the human's own.

1. **The conversation already happens.** Their words at [The human rejects *most efforts never
   end*](86-most-efforts-never-end-rejected.md), `verbatim`: *"If it is a very long running effort it will be talked
   about during the start of the effort I expect."* This writes down an act rather than adding one.
2. **The fact already belongs to the destination.** [Done-ness and termination](10-done-ness.md) rules that *"the
   destination decides whether an effort can terminate"*, and its own taxonomy separates bounded delivery efforts
   that *"empty, terminate, and get closed"* from open-ended ones that may never empty. This states at the
   destination what the destination was already deciding, instead of leaving it to be inferred.
3. **Nothing else can carry it.** With no frequency claim anywhere, per-effort knowledge is the only thing left that
   can tell a manager whether this effort's frontier is expected to empty — and this effort's own standing rule is
   that anything held only in the manager's context dies with its thread. *"It was discussed at the start"* is not a
   record.

### What turns on it

The node's question named three things. One is decided elsewhere and two fall out here.

- **Whether flags may wait for the closing report** — decided against waiting by [Whether a research node's and an
  AFK precondition node's flags settle at the pick report
  too](102-do-afk-nodes-flags-settle-at-the-pick-report.md), for every kind of node. Nothing here re-opens it.
- **Whether an empty frontier is a milestone or a surprise** — it is a **milestone** where the effort is expected to
  end: the thing that triggers the whole-graph sweep and the report preceding a close. It is a **surprise** worth
  reporting as such where the effort is not.
- **Whether the closing report is a destination or an exception path** — a destination on an effort expected to end,
  an exception path on one that is not.

### This still makes no claim about frequency

What is recorded is one effort's own expectation, stated by the party charting it. The workflow continues to say
nothing about how often efforts end, in any wording, and a manager still may not present a non-empty frontier as
failure — which node 86 settled and this does not touch.

### Where this answer's write lands

Not on a node of its own. Its two clauses ride the implementation node reported on
[node 98](98-starting-an-effort-has-no-section.md)'s handover, *Write the starting-an-effort section and its
procedure* — items 4, 6, 9 and 10 of its acceptance condition — because the prose is the same prose and the files are
the same two files.

**One thing this effort owes itself.** `map.md`'s own `## Destination` says what reaching the end looks like —
*"Reached when the workflow has been run end to end on at least one real effort"* — and does not say whether reaching
it is expected. Under node 10's taxonomy this effort is bounded and is expected to end. Saying so is one line under
the `Change the map` procedure, and it is the manager's.

> **The manager's note, not a change to the answer above.** Done at the moment of filing, under `Change the map`:
> this effort's `## Destination` now carries that line.
